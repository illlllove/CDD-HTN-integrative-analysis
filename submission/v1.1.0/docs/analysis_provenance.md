# Analysis provenance

## Purpose

This document records the provenance and interpretation of the frozen
derived results deposited for the Human Genomics submission.

## Candidate reconstruction

The archived shared-screening analysis comprised 15,508 jointly evaluable
expression probes.

The exploratory shared-screen statistic was defined as the maximum of the
two outcome-specific SMR P values.

Among the jointly evaluable probes, 412 satisfied the nominal exploratory
shared-screen criterion.

None survived Benjamini-Hochberg false-discovery-rate correction.

The archived primary candidate-retention rule required H4 >= 0.50 in all
three pairwise colocalization comparisons under p12 = 1e-5:

1. cis-eQTL versus hypertension;
2. cis-eQTL versus cervical disc disorders;
3. hypertension versus cervical disc disorders.

Application of this rule retained four transcript hypotheses:

- NT5C2
- CNNM2
- MARCKSL1P1
- RMC1

NT5C2, CNNM2 and MARCKSL1P1 map to the same chromosome 10 candidate
region and are treated as competing transcript hypotheses rather than
independent genetic discoveries.

RMC1 represents the chromosome 18 locus-specific follow-up hypothesis.

## Figure 2

Figure 2 provides post hoc regional characterization of the two retained
candidate regions.

Variant-level SNP.PP.H4 values are interpreted as posterior probabilities
conditional on the H4 hypothesis.

The 95% conditional H4 credible sets were generated by ordering variants
by SNP.PP.H4 and accumulating posterior probability until at least 0.95
was reached.

These analyses characterize regional compatibility and do not establish a
resolved shared causal SNP.

## Figure 3

Figure 3 evaluates sensitivity to the assumed shared-variant prior.

Applying the identical three-pair H4 >= 0.50 rule to the complete archived
412-probe batch retained:

- 0 candidates at p12 = 1e-6;
- 4 candidates at p12 = 1e-5;
- 15 candidates at p12 = 5e-5.

Formal four-candidate analyses likewise showed substantial prior
sensitivity.

The results therefore support prior-sensitive regional compatibility rather
than prior-invariant evidence of shared causality.

## Figure 4

Figure 4 evaluates external genetic context.

Million Veteran Program systolic blood pressure data provide regional
cardiovascular concordance.

These results are not interpreted as transcript-specific replication or
phenotype-matched replication of the discovery hypertension GWAS.

UK Biobank M50 provides secondary CDD-side sensitivity evidence and did
not provide additional colocalization support for the retained candidate
regions.

This finding is not interpreted as definitive failed replication because
phenotype definition, statistical power and variant coverage differ from
the primary FinnGen analysis.

## Reconstruction scripts

The deposited Figure 2-4 reconstruction scripts were generated specifically
for the Human Genomics submission release from frozen derived result tables.

They are provided to demonstrate reproducible transformation of the
deposited derived tables into figure-level summaries.

They are not represented as complete original historical analysis scripts.

## Historical chronology

The available archived records support reconstruction of candidate
membership and analytical settings but do not independently establish the
prospective timing of every threshold or the exact execution order of every
external or sensitivity analysis.

No stronger claim regarding historical chronology is made by this release.
