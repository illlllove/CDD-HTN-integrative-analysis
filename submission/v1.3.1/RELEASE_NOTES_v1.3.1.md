# Release notes — v1.3.1

This submission-final update extends the frozen Human Genomics reproducibility archive with the analyses added after v1.1.0.

## Added or updated

- Formal regional colocalization between the discovery hypertension GWAS and MVP systolic blood pressure (MVP-SBP) across the p12 sensitivity grid.
- Updated Figure 4 integrating formal HTN–MVP-SBP colocalization, descriptive regional Z-score correlation, and post hoc UKB M50 sensitivity.
- Updated Figure 1 panel c distinguishing chromosome 10 H4-favored from chromosome 18 H3-dominant MVP-SBP evidence.
- Post hoc eQTL quantitative-trait sdY sensitivity analysis at 0.8, 1.0, and 1.2.
- Supplementary Tables S1–S2.

## Key archived results

At the reference prior p12 = 1e-5, HTN–MVP-SBP colocalization gave PP.H3/PP.H4 = 0.0840/0.9160 at chromosome 10 and 0.9359/0.00425 at chromosome 18. Across the eight eQTL–trait sdY sensitivity comparisons, the maximum full-precision H4 range was 6.011326e-4, the maximum absolute deviation from sdY = 1 was 3.991589e-4, and no comparison crossed the H4 = 0.50 compatibility threshold.

## Reproducibility boundary

Raw third-party datasets and controlled-access MVP summary statistics are not redistributed. This archive contains derived summaries, figures, scripts, and supplementary tables supporting the submission-final analyses.
